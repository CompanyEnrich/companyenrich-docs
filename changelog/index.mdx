Follow the evolution of CompanyEnrich, from our first public release to new ways to find, enrich, and understand companies and people.

These announcements describe features at the time of release. See the API reference for current endpoint behavior and pricing.

## March 25, 2026

### [Company Headcount API](/changelog/company-headcount-api)

Added Company Headcount API to the platform, enabling historical employee count tracking and department-level workforce analysis for any company. This feature surfaces growth signals, layoff patterns, and hiring trends over time — allowing users to monitor workforce changes, benchmark company size, and build data-driven prospecting workflows around team growth and contraction.

## February 26, 2026

### [Reverse Email Lookup API](/changelog/reverse-email-lookup-api)

Added Reverse Email Lookup endpoint to the API, enabling professional profile discovery from a single email address. Submit any work or personal email and receive enriched data including the person's full name, current job title, company, and location, making it effortless to identify and qualify contacts, enrich CRM records, and automate lead enrichment pipelines.

## February 18, 2026

### [People Search API](/changelog/people-search-api)

Added People Search API to the platform, enabling discovery and enrichment of professional profiles based on targeted search criteria. This feature allows users to search for individuals by name, job title, seniority level, department, or company, and returns enriched profile data including current role, location, and professional background, making it easy to build prospect lists, verify contacts, and power people-based workflows.

## September 7, 2025

### [Autocomplete Companies Endpoint](/changelog/autocomplete-companies-api)

Added companies autocomplete endpoint to the API, returning companies and logos matching partial domain name inputs. This endpoint enables domain name autocompletion functionality, providing company suggestions based on partial domain matches for integration into search and form interfaces.

## August 5, 2025

### [Preview Endpoints](/changelog/preview-endpoints)

Added /preview endpoints to the Similar Companies API and Company Search API, enabling free query testing without consuming credits. Endpoints return up to 25 results at no cost, allowing users to experiment with filters, refine search parameters, and validate query logic without credit consumption.

## July 25, 2025

### [Company Funding Data](/changelog/company-funding-data)

Added comprehensive funding information to the API, enabling detailed financial insights and investment tracking for companies. This feature provides access to funding rounds, investment amounts, investor details, and funding stages, allowing users to analyze company growth and financial backing.

## June 16, 2025

### [Technology Stack Search](/changelog/technology-stack-search)

Added technology stack search functionality to the API, enabling companies to be discovered based on their technology infrastructure and tools. This feature allows filtering by specific technologies like React, Salesforce, AWS, or broader categories such as CRM, Analytics, and E-commerce platforms.

## May 16, 2025

### [Keyword Search](/changelog/keyword-search)

Added keyword search functionality to the API, enabling precise company discovery through targeted keyword matching across company descriptions, business activities, and industry classification

## April 25, 2025

### [Semantic Search](/changelog/semantic-search)

Added semantic search functionality to the API, enabling natural language queries for company discovery. This AI-powered feature matches companies based on conceptual understanding rather than exact keyword matching, improving search accuracy and relevance.

## March 29, 2025

### [Exclude Filters](/changelog/exclude-filters)

Added exclusion filters to the API, allowing users to exclude companies that match specific criteria. This feature enables more precise company data by filtering out unwanted company profiles based on customizable parameters.

## December 19, 2024

### [Instant Company Enrichment](/changelog/instant-company-enrichment)

Now our API enriching new company domains in real-time. When you request a domain that isn't in our database, we'll enrich it instantly upon request instead of queueing it for later processing.

## December 6, 2024

### [Introducing Our Free Logo API](/changelog/introducing-our-free-logo-api)

We just launched a Free Logo API. You can now integrate company logos into your applications effortlessly and for free. Visit [dedicated page](https://companyenrich.com/logo-api) for more information and to get started.
