## Overview

Review where **Company Enrich** gets its data, how we handle privacy and security, and how you can request data access or deletion.

***

## Data Sources

We combine public sources with proprietary sources (data that providers own or control). Our data sources include:

* **Public Databases**: We combine information from verified public records, regulatory filings, and data registries.
* **Partnered Data Providers**: We use information from vetted third-party partners to enrich and verify company insights.
* **User-Generated Data**: We use information that users submit, when applicable, with their explicit consent.
* **Open Web Data**: We collect data from publicly available websites through automated extraction (web scraping), following data scraping guidelines and legal requirements.

We routinely update and validate our data to maintain its accuracy and relevance.

***

## Compliance Standards

**Company Enrich** follows these privacy and security standards and practices:

* **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**: Our platform aligns with GDPR standards to respect the data privacy rights of individuals in the EU. We handle personal information transparently, securely, and within legal boundaries.
* **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)**: We respect the data rights of California residents, complying with CCPA requirements to protect personal data, facilitate data access requests, and manage data deletion upon request.
* **Data Minimization**: We limit the data we collect and process to what our services require. We also limit how much personal information we expose and how long we retain it.
* **User Consent**: We obtain explicit consent before using user-submitted information. We aim to make our data protection practices clear to users and contributors.
* **Security Protocols**: We protect our platform with data encryption, regular security audits, and access controls that restrict unauthorized access.

***

## Ethical Data Use

Our policies and procedures aim to prevent misuse of information and promote responsible data practices. We do not engage in or support practices that involve:

* Selling personal data without consent.
* Using data for discriminatory profiling or purposes.
* Gathering data from restricted sources.

Our goal is to support businesses while respecting the privacy and rights of individuals.

***

## Contact & Data Requests

Contact our Data Compliance Team at **[support@companyenrich.com](mailto:support@companyenrich.com)** to:

* Ask about our data sources or compliance policies.
* Request access to your data.
* Request deletion of your data.

***

## FAQs

* **Where does your data come from?**
  We collect data from public records, vetted data partners, user submissions, and publicly available websites. We follow applicable data laws and obtain explicit consent for user-submitted information.

* **How often is the data updated?**
  We regularly refresh and validate our data.

* **How do I know my company’s data is protected?**
  We use data encryption, regular security audits, and access controls to protect your data and privacy.
